FAITHFUL HEARTS: A POEM ABOUT DOGS

In every corner of this world they dwell,
With wagging tails and stories they could tell,
These noble creatures, loyal and so true,
With eyes that sparkle as they look at you.

From tiny paws to massive, gentle frames,
In countless colors, breeds, and different names,
They bound through life with grace and endless cheer,
A steadfast presence, always drawing near.

Their devotion runs as deep as any sea,
More constant than the steadiest oak tree,
They greet you not with judgment, spite, or shame,
But pure acceptance, again and again the same.

With tails that wag like flags of pure delight,
They chase the day and dream away the night,
They play with hearts so open and so free,
And give us joy in all that they could be.

They stand as guardians against the dark,
Their love a fierce and never-failing spark,
A comfort when the world brings us to tears,
A strength that carries us through all our fears.

They celebrate our victories, both small and grand,
And by our side forever faithfully they stand,
No words are needed for this bond so rare,
Just presence, touch, and love beyond compare.

O blessed dogs, with hearts of purest gold,
You teach us lessons that will never grow old,
That loyalty and love need not be earned or bought,
That simple joy is life's most precious thought.

So here's to every dog who walks this earth,
Who fills our lives with laughter, warmth, and worth,
Forever in our hearts you'll always stay,
Our faithful friends, from yesterday to every day.
