OUR FAITHFUL FRIENDS

In every corner of this world they walk,
With wagging tails and eager hearts,
The guardians of our earliest joys,
The menders of our breaking parts.

Across the ages, through the years,
They've stood beside us, true and strong,
In diverse forms—from great to small—
To them our spirits all belong.

Their eyes reflect a boundless love,
No judgment found within that gaze,
Just pure devotion, warm and deep,
A constant flame through all our days.

With graceful bounds they leap and play,
Their energy ignites our own,
Yet when we sit in darkest hours,
They sense the pain we've never shown.

They teach us how to live right now,
To find joy in the smallest things,
To greet each day as if it's new,
To feel the freedom movement brings.

From mighty beasts to gentle souls,
They guard us with their courage true,
And ask so little in return—
Just love, and trust, and me and you.

In them we find our truest friend,
A family member through and through,
No words are needed, bonds run deep,
A timeless bond forever true.

So here's to those with beating hearts,
Who give us more than we can measure,
Our faithful friends forevermore,
Our greatest source of joy and pleasure.
